Nova D1 Simulator

The device's own software, running in this page.

This is the Nova D1 package for RPCortex v2, compiled to WebAssembly from the same C++ the device runs. The panel below is the display chip's memory, filled the way the real driver fills it, so what it shows is what the OLED would show. Turn the knob, press the buttons, open any app.

The radios, GPS, sensors and files are simulated, and the Wi-Fi networks and nearby devices are made up. The screens, the navigation and the settings are the real thing.
